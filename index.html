Basketball rules

Basics

Playtime: 4x 10 Minutes

the goal of the game is to score points

Teams

2 Teams with 5 Player each

max. 7 bench players pro team

Points

Type of throw Points
free throw 1 point
normal throw 2 points
three-point throw 3 points

Time rules

name Description
Shot clock An attack may last a 24 Seconds. The clock starts after a throw-in and resets as soon as the attack stops
8 Second rule If the team recives the ball, they must transport the ball from their own half in the half of the other team
5 Second rule When throwing in, a player may only hold the ball for max. 5 Seconds. If a player is guarded, in the game, he must start dribbling, hand over the ball or make a point
3 Second rule During an attack, the deffender may be in the enemy zone for max. 3 seconds
