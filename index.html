Basketball Rules

The most important rules

Basics

⏱️

Playtime

4 × 10 minutes
with breaks in between

🎯

Goal

Score more points than the other team

⏸️

Breaks

2 min. between quarters
15 min. at halftime

Teams & Player

👥

On the field

2 teams with 5 players each on the field at the same time

🪑

Bank

Maximum of 7 substitutes per team (squad normaly 12)

🔄

Substitutions

Unlimited possibile – during breaks in play

🚫

Disqualification

After 5 fouls, a player must leave the field.

Point allocation

1

after a foul
2
normal throw
inside the three-point line
3
three point throw
behind the three-point line

Time rules

24s

Shot Clock

An attack may last a maximum of 24 seconds. The clock starts after the throw-in and is reset when the ball touches the rim or the other team gains possession of the ball.

8s

8-Second-Rule

Upon gaining possession, the ball must be moved from one's own half into the opponent's half within 8 seconds.

5s

5-Second-Rule

Hold the ball for a maximum of 5 seconds during a throw-in. A closely guarded player must dribble, pass, or shoot within 5 seconds.

3s

3-Second-Rule

An attacking player may remain in the opposing restricted area (the zone under the basket) for a maximum of 3 consecutive seconds.

Other important rules

🏀

Double dribble

After stopping the dribble, the ball must not be dribbled again.

👟

Steps

More than 2 steps witheout dribbling are not allowed

✋

Fouls

Personal, unsportsmanlike, and technical fouls. You are out after five personal fouls.

📏

Out of the field

The ball is out of play if it touches the sideline or baseline, or if a player touches it outside the court.
